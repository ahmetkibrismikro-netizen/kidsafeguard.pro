After successful payment, your license key is shown instantly on screen AND emailed to your inbox. In the extension, click "⭐ Activate Pro", enter the license code and your email, and all Pro features unlock immediately.
Can my child turn off the protection? +
No. The extension requires your secret parent password to disable protection. Children cannot uninstall extensions or bypass the password. The protection works even when Chrome is restarted.
What exactly does it block? +
KidSafe Guard Pro blocks: adult/pornographic websites, gambling and casino sites, adult live cam sites, explicit chat platforms (Omegle, Chatroulette), violent content sites, and dark web links. YouTube is forced into Restricted Mode and search engines are locked to SafeSearch.
Does it work when I'm not at home? +
Yes! KidSafe Guard Pro works entirely in the browser — it doesn't need an internet connection to your home network. As long as Chrome is open and protection is enabled, your child is protected anywhere.
What if my child opens a different browser? +
KidSafe Guard Pro works in Chrome. We recommend setting Chrome as the only accessible browser, or installing the extension in every browser your child might use. You can also restrict other browsers via Windows/Mac parental controls.
